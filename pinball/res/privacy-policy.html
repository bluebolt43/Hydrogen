Pinball Tales Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy applies to Pinball Tales (彈珠物語), Android application ID com.chickeneren43.pinballtales.

Information Collection and Sharing

The game content runs locally on your device. The app does not require you to create a game account or upload saved progress to a developer-operated server. It does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play processes in-app purchases and related information as described below.

When you open the announcement window, the app requests the announcement page from GitHub Pages over HTTPS. Opening this privacy policy also connects to GitHub Pages through your browser. GitHub receives connection information such as your IP address and request metadata to serve these pages, under its own privacy statement. The app does not attach saved progress or purchase records to these requests, or load remote tracking scripts, images, or fonts in the announcement window. Announcement requests are not made during ordinary offline gameplay.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores progress, high scores, language, sound preferences, tutorial status, and other game settings locally on your device. This data is used to resume gameplay and remember your preferences. It is not uploaded to the developer.

The app also stores verified Google Play purchase receipts in app-private local storage. These records can include product IDs, purchase tokens, order IDs when provided by Google Play, purchase status, and receipt signatures. They are used to verify permanent table access and retain previously verified access during offline play. The app does not upload these receipts to a developer-operated server.

Local progress and settings remain until reset in the game, cleared through Android app storage settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled. The local purchase cache is refreshed when Google Play successfully returns current purchase information, and is removed when app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Resetting game progress does not cancel purchases or erase Google Play's purchase history.

Device Features

The game uses vibration, where available and enabled, for gameplay feedback, and can adjust device audio volume in response to your controls. These features do not collect personal information. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Google Play Purchases

The app offers optional, one-time purchases for permanent access to paid tables. Google Play handles checkout, payment processing, and the Google account associated with your purchases. The app does not receive or store your full credit card number or payment credentials. Google processes payment and account information under Google's Privacy Policy.

The app uses Google Play Billing to request product information and prices, launch purchases, query purchase ownership, and acknowledge completed purchases. It verifies purchase receipts on your device before granting access. When an internet connection is available, ownership is checked at startup, when returning to the app, and after the purchase flow. Successful checks update local access, including changes such as refunds or revoked purchases. A failed connection does not by itself erase previously verified offline access.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the local receipt cache but does not cancel permanent purchases recorded by Google Play. To restore access after reinstalling or changing devices, connect to Google Play using the Google account that made the purchase. The game does not provide cloud synchronization of game progress. Google Play may make order information available to the developer through Play Console for order management, refunds, and support.

Contacting the Developer

If you email the developer, your email provider transmits your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. The developer uses this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues, and retains it only as long as needed for those purposes. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing chickeneren43@gmail.com. Your email provider processes messages under its own privacy policy.

External Services

If you choose to open an external link, the destination website or app handles information under its own privacy policy. Google Play and Android may separately process information under Google's policies. This policy describes the app's own handling of data and the external services it uses. The developer does not sell your personal information.

Children's Privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact the email address below to request deletion.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The latest policy and its revision date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact chickeneren43@gmail.com.

App updates

At app startup, this app asks Google Play whether an update is available for your account. When you choose to update, Google Play opens and handles downloading and installing the update. If the check fails or no update is available, you can continue playing.
